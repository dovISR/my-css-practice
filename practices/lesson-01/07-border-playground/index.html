📦 Border Playground

Six colored boxes are already on the page. Nothing outlines them yet. Each border you add should be easy to point at.

Your mission

  1. Draw a solid line around the blue box.
  2. Draw a dashed line around the orange box.
  3. Draw a dotted line around the green box.
  4. Make the purple line much thicker than the others.
  5. Pick a border color you can see on each background.
  6. Round the corners of the teal box.
  7. Draw a double line around the pink box.

Solid Border

One continuous line.

Dashed Border

Even breaks in the line.

Dotted Border

A line made of dots.

Thick Border

The same kind of line, drawn much heavier.

Rounded Border

The corners soften. The line is still there.

Double Border

Two lines, with a gap between them.
