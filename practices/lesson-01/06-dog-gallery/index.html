Lesson 01 · Exercise 06

🐶 The Dog Gallery

Six dogs, four ways to arrange a box. The colors, photos, and type are already done. You decide how the boxes sit.

Your mission

  1. Stack the cards in the Block lab.
  2. Sit the Inline-block cards in a row and keep their size.
  3. Set the Inline cards to inline and watch the 200px size disappear.
  4. Make the dashed tray a flex row. Flex goes on the tray, not on the dogs.
  5. Put space between the cards in that tray.

What changes

Block starts a new line and keeps a width.

Inline-block sits beside its neighbors and keeps a width.

Inline sits beside its neighbors and ignores width and height.

Flex is written on the parent. The children line up, and gap is the space between them.
